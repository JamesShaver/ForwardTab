Eliminating Food Insecurity,
One Meal at a Time.

Trust Tab is a 501(c)(3) digital escrow platform that connects public generosity and corporate CSR sponsorships with local independent restaurants to fund pre-paid meals for neighbors in need—with zero intake and full dignity.

No spam. We’ll only notify you about local pilot launches and launch milestones.

100% Tax Deductible

Patron micro-donations and corporate CSR sponsorships flow directly into a firewalled 501(c)(3) central escrow account with automated tax receipting.

Fair Market Value

Participating local restaurants receive automated Stripe Connect ACH transfers at 100% full menu price, supporting local small businesses.

Zero-Intake Dignity

Meal recipients claim physical tickets from counter boards with no paperwork, qualifying questions, or stigma—just a warm meal served with respect.
